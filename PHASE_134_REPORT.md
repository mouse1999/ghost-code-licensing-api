## 5. Lifecycle and concurrency (executed on PostgreSQL)
Covered by the 118 tests, `Phase134LifecycleDesktopTest` and the contract run: (1) issue only with payment confirmed; (2) portal open/review/decline creates nothing; (3) preset term counted from activation (issued 2 days earlier, 3-day term measured from activation); (4) fixed expiry survives activation and is exactly the signed entitlement's bound; (5) fixed-expiry pending license cannot be activated after its expiry (`LICENSE_STATUS`, no device registered); (6) renewal by days and by exact date; (7) suspended stays suspended after renewal until reinstated; (8) revoked and never-activated cannot be renewed (409 `ILLEGAL_TRANSITION`), revoked cannot be reinstated; (9) an expired license returns to active after renewal on the **same installed device with no reactivation**; (10) suspend/reinstate/revoke are reflected by authoritative validation and by the signed-entitlement endpoint; (11) every successful mutation has its audit event, refused ones are audited as failures. Time is a controllable clock (no sleeps).
